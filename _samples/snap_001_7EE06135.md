## 2. FLEET TARGET — up to 50 subagents RUNNING

**Target: 50 live lanes, not 50 dispatched over time.** "Succeeded" is not "running".

    python scripts/subagent-inspect.py --since 30m --limit 60   # lanes seen in the last 30m
    python scripts/suborch-fleet.py cap --max-concurrency 50

The cap is **PROVEN ENFORCED, not advisory**: `--max-concurrency 50` → `EFFECTIVE cap = 50`
rc=0; `--max-concurrency 999` → **rc=2 REFUSED**. Never relax it past 50.

**This is CONFIG-FILE ARITHMETIC, not a live-capability measurement**: `suborch-fleet.py:356-357` reads the configured cap from `config.yaml` and computes EFFECTIVE = min(configured, requested); the rc=2 at `suborch-fleet.py:367-369` is argv compared against that file. The runtime's real ceiling is found only by dispatching and reading its refusal — never from this number.

Dispatch in batches and keep going until the target is reached or a ceiling is measured and
reported. **If the runtime's own child-concurrency limit refuses the batch, that number is
your real ceiling — report it as a measurement with its POPULATION, do not report it as a
goal you failed.** Naming the ceiling IS the deliverable when the ceiling is the truth.

A lane only counts as running when a run/ledger row exists for it. A `task` that returned
"spawned" is a **declaration**; the response is the **measurement**. Report the two apart.
