      Pause and unload?

      Pausing unloads the live caption engine and the background transcript. RAM and CPU are given back, and nothing is transcribed until you resume.

      Worth knowing: the background transcript is extremely light — it costs almost nothing to leave running. Pausing is not necessary to keep the PC fast, and resuming reloads the model and takes a few seconds.
