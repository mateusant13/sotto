8. **DECISION (recorded, not yet measured):** **ship EmbeddingGemma 2**, default tier **text+vision 440M** (Parakeet owns speech, OCR is the third channel; the 300M audio encoder buys non-speech sound retrieval for +340 MB q8 / +587 MB fp16), index at **256d**, **one `encode()` per 5 s window** fed with frames from our own WGC tap (never the MP4), pragmatic Windows runtime **llama.cpp GGUF** (text Q8_0 310 MB + mmproj Q8_0 555 MB), reference runtime `sentence-transformers ≥6.1.0` + `transformers ≥5.19` + `torchcodec`. All alternatives that exist are worse on coverage or licence: Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B/8B (apache-2.0, **no audio**, 3–10× weights), llama-nemotron-embed-vl-1b-v2 (licence `other`), jina-embeddings-v4 (**licence UNKNOWN here** — HF declares none, `?blobs=true` → 401), CLAP (apache-2.0, audio only, second index).
