What type of capture features does Highlights support?
Games that support Highlights automatically capture key moments, clutch kills, and match-winning plays, ensuring that your best gaming moments are automatically saved. For games that do not support automatic Highlights, you can still record instant replays of your last few moments of gameplay, manual video recordings, or screenshots.
