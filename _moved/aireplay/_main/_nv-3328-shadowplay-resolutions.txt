What resolution does ShadowPlay support?
Updated 10/19/2021 01:55 PM
ShadowPlay can record at the following resolutions. If the gameplay aspect ratio is different, black bars are added to preserve the original aspect ratio of the captured footage.
Resolution:
In-game
4320p 8K
2160p 4K
1440P HD
1080p HD
720p HD
480p
360p
