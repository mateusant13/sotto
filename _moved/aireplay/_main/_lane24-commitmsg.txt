wake: apply the six receipt-21 defects + D-7 (wrong transport predictor)

receipt-21 (lane16) audited the wake machinery and reported six defects
read-only. This applies them, and adds a seventh the orchestrator measured
after the brief was written.

D-1 userMessageId shape -> mint 'msg-user-v1-' + token_urlsafe(32) = 55 chars,
   the measured runtime shape. NOT a delivery fix: two role='user' rows
   already reached a session carrying our 48-char ids.
D-2 cron query -> count cron FIELDS, not stars. Both the shipped
   like '%* * * * *%' AND receipt-21's proposed like '%* * * *' return 0 of 30.
D-3 dedupe wedge -> an expired row is not pending and cannot refuse a wake;
   the refusal now names the blocking item, age, claim and expiry.
D-4 dead cron alarm -> cron-alarm, rc=3 past 15 min while a cron is armed.
   Firing in production on the live 27 h outage.
D-5 orphans -> session-wide sweep (60 min foreign floor) AND expires_at_ms on
   insert; neither alone is demonstrable, so both.
D-6 shutdown deadline -> counted, not described. N=6, no rate claimed.
D-7 local_runtime_sessions.status does NOT predict exec acceptance
   (false negative on 2 of 2 refusals). Branch on the turn lease + a bounded
   open turn, and fall back to the queue whenever exec is refused anyway.

Two of receipt-21's numbers did not survive re-measurement; both are recorded
in the receipt rather than quietly dropped.
