EXCLUSIONS FROM ARM-3 (printed so they cannot hide):
  ARM-3 is LINE-scoped by design: a line containing the word 'measured' must carry
  an ISO date on that same line.  There is NO allowlist of exempted lines -- a
  blanket exemption is how a 'measured with no date' claim would survive.  Lines
  that merely NAME the bad 2026-10-08 stamp (the correction note at the top of the
  file) contain no word 'measured', so ARM-3/ARM-4 never see them.

  ARM-4 exempts nothing either: it only fires on lines that also assert a measurement.
